NEWTON'S 1ST LAW: INERTIA ΣF = 0 ⟹ v = const

In the frictionless vacuum of deep space, an object in motion preserves its velocity vector indefinitely unless acted upon by an external net force.

  • In Game: When you stop steering, your ship does not instantly freeze; it glides smoothly along its inertia vector.
  • Retro-thrusters: Computer-controlled RCS thrusters apply counter-force for controlled orbital maneuvers.

NEWTON'S 2ND LAW: DYNAMICS F = m·a = dp/dt

Acceleration is directly proportional to net force and inversely proportional to ship mass.

  • In Game: Ion thrust generates continuous acceleration ($\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{F}_{thrust} / m$).
  • Momentum (p = mv): Shown in your HUD combat panel in Newton-seconds ($N\cdot s$).

NEWTON'S 3RD LAW: REACTION F_A = -F_B

Every action produces an equal and opposite reaction. Mutual forces between interacting bodies are identical in magnitude and opposite in direction.

  • In Game: Blaster discharge generates micro-recoil kicks in the crosshair HUD.
  • Collisions: When asteroids shatter, 14 debris rock fragments disperse symmetrically, conserving net center-of-mass momentum.

UNIVERSAL GRAVITATION & ORBITS Fg = -G·M·m / r²

Every massive celestial body exerts an attractive force proportional to mass product and inversely proportional to the square of distance.

  • Circular Velocity: $v_{orb} = \sqrt{G M / r}$ keeps you in stable orbit.
  • Escape Velocity: $v_{esc} = \sqrt{2 G M / r}$ is the parabolic speed required to escape the planet's gravitational well into hyperspace.

EINSTEIN'S GENERAL RELATIVITY G_μν = (8πG/c⁴) T_μν

Mass and energy curve four-dimensional spacetime. Gravity is not a magical invisible pull, but matter following the straightest possible paths (geodesics) through curved spacetime.

  • In Game: In Sector 5 and near Jupiter, the wireframe grid physically bends downward into an Einstein gravitational funnel.

RELATIVISTIC DOPPLER EFFECT f_obs = f_0 √((1+β)/(1-β))

As a spacecraft approaches relativistic velocities ($\beta = v/c$), electromagnetic radiation shifts frequency:

  • Blueshift: Stars in your forward flight path shift towards shorter wavelengths (blue/cyan).
  • Redshift: Stars in your wake shift towards longer wavelengths (crimson/red).

SOLAR FLUX & THE GLYPH Φ Φ = L_☉ / (4π r²)

Why is our starfighter shaped like the Greek letter Φ? In physics, $\Phi$ designates Radiant Energy Flux ($W/m^2$).

  • Hexagonal Solar Wings: The dual vertical arrays are massive photovoltaic collectors gathering stellar irradiance to power the twin ion propulsion manifolds.
  • Inverse Square Law: Flux drops with $1/r^2$: from $63,200\text{ W/m}^2$ at the Sun to $50\text{ W/m}^2$ at Jupiter.

EVENT HORIZON & TIDAL FORCES r_s = 2GM / c²

A gravitational singularity surrounded by an Event Horizon ($r_s$) where escape velocity exceeds the speed of light ($c$).

  • Tidal Force Gradient: $dF/dr = 2GMm/r^3$ stretches matter along the radial vector (spaghettification).
  • In Game (Sector 5): You must orient directly OUTWARD and fire maximum thrusters ($W$) before crossing the horizon!
